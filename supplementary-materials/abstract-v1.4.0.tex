\begin{abstract}
\noindent
This supplement is the analytical companion to \emph{Frame covariance of CMBR
lensing in the exact $1+3$ covariant radiation hierarchy} (Gebbie, Paper~1,
\texttt{arXiv:astro-ph/9912072v4}; the suffix is load-bearing --- \texttt{v4}
is Paper~1, \texttt{v3} the correction, \texttt{v1}--\texttt{v2} the 1999
originals). It documents an independent Python reconstruction, from
first $1+3$ covariant principles, of the almost-Friedmann--Lema\^itre results
of Gebbie, Dunsby \& Ellis, \emph{Annals of Physics} \textbf{282}, 321--394
(2000), written bottom-up and not ported from a Boltzmann code.

\medskip\noindent
It carries the derivations the bundle rests on --- the exact hierarchy, the
source closing at rank two, the frame law, the acoustic oscillator, free
streaming, the matching and integral solution, and the angular spectrum ---
each tied to the gate that tests it, with the open questions and the
approximations collected in one place.

\medskip\noindent
Four claims. The antecedent's equations, re-derived and evaluated
independently, reproduce what the antecedent says they do, and where they do
not the disagreement is located, named and measured: fifteen findings
against the antecedent and forty-eight against this bundle. Multipoles of rank
two and above are frame-invariant at first order, and both released frames
come from one solve. The released integral solution~(102) is not the exact
integration by parts of its own integral, and the discrepancy is itemised in
four rows. The first acoustic peak does not yet sit where a matched external
spectrum puts it, and the residual has no established mechanism.

\medskip\noindent
This is not a calibration, a fit, or a claim of a new physical effect; the
1999 claim of one was withdrawn and nothing here reinstates it. Distance to
CAMB and CLASS is measured and itemised by approximation, not minimised. One
limit ships stated rather than discovered: the recombination grid sets the
primary source at $\eta_*$ and nothing derives it, worth $1.2\%$ --- larger
than every tolerance claimed here, and invisible to the acceptance gate.
\end{abstract}
