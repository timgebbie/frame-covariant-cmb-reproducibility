\begin{abstract}
\noindent
This supplement is the analytical companion to \emph{Frame covariance of CMBR
lensing in the exact $1+3$ covariant radiation hierarchy} (Gebbie, Paper~1,
\texttt{arXiv:astro-ph/9912072v4}; the suffix is load-bearing: \texttt{v4} is
Paper~1, \texttt{v3} the correction, \texttt{v1}--\texttt{v2} the 1999
originals). It documents an independent Python reconstruction, from first
$1+3$ covariant principles, of the almost-Friedmann--Lema\^itre results of
Gebbie, Dunsby \& Ellis, \emph{Annals of Physics} \textbf{282}, 321--394
(2000), written bottom-up and not ported from a Boltzmann code.
\textbf{Part~I} states where the work stands and what is open; \textbf{Part~II}
carries the derivations the bundle rests on --- the exact hierarchy, the source
closing at rank two, the frame law, the acoustic oscillator, free streaming,
the matching and integral solution, and the angular spectrum --- each tied to
the gate that tests it; \textbf{Part~III} is the qualification evidence. Four
claims. The antecedent's equations, re-derived and evaluated independently,
reproduce what the antecedent says they do, and where they do not it is
located, named and measured: fifteen findings against the antecedent and
forty-eight against this bundle. Multipoles of rank two and above are
frame-invariant at first order, and both released frames come from one solve.
The released integral solution~(102) is not the exact integration by parts of
its own integral, and the discrepancy is itemised in four rows. The first
acoustic peak does not sit where a matched external spectrum puts it and the
Sachs--Wolfe plateau is absent; both are localised and neither is repaired.
This is not a calibration, a fit, or a claim of new physics; the 1999 claim of
one was withdrawn and nothing here reinstates it. Distance to CAMB and CLASS is
measured and itemised by approximation, not minimised.
\end{abstract}
