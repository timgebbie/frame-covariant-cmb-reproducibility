# F10 — the angular autocorrelation as it forms

**The standalone caption.** Open this one to see *when* the spectrum is built.

## How to read the two panels

**(a)** is the autocorrelation you would measure at each epoch, normalised once
— by the maximum of the final curve — so the panel shows growth and shape
together.

**(b)** is the same data as a surface: multipole up the side, conformal time
across, shade the fraction of today's value. The low rows keep changing well to
the right of recombination, which is the late integrated Sachs–Wolfe effect.

## What it reads

| | |
|---|---|
| late share at ℓ = 2, 3, 4 | **+0.54, +0.37, +0.22** |
| late share at ℓ = 5–12 | **−0.03 to −0.51** (late power cancels earlier power) |
| late share above ℓ = 30 | **+0.05 to +0.21** |
| η₀ column against the full integral | 1.0e-13 |

The late share is the fraction of today's C_ℓ laid down after
(η − η\*) = 0.01 (η₀ − η\*), when the visibility has closed.
