# F9 — the angular spectrum with the acoustic peaks, in both frames

**The standalone caption.** The spectrum this bundle computes from the 1+3
covariant line of sight, for three backgrounds, in two frames.

## What it shows

| | EdS | ΛCDM | Planck |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| first peak, ℓ | 160 | 140 | 206 |
| largest frame gap, any ℓ | 2.9e-14 | 3.3e-14 | 3.3e-14 |

On Planck the potential is solved with the CDM, the baryons and the photons
from one start deep in the radiation era (the radiation era's construction,
steps 1 to 3), and the even peaks stand beside the odd ones: by the shared
prominence rule the maxima are at ℓ = 210, 480 and 720 and the first interior
minimum at 360, CAMB's on the same grid, with the first maximum at 205.5
against CAMB's 206.1. Below ℓ ≈ 30 the curve lies within 1.8 per cent of
CAMB's shape. On
Einstein–de Sitter and ΛCDM, which have no radiation era, the even peaks are
absent: with no radiation-era driving the oscillation's zero point is set by
baryon loading, which suppresses the rarefaction peaks to a few per cent of the
compression peaks. That is approximation **A-3**, and it is upstream of the
line of sight.

## What it does not say

* **It does not say the heights are right.** On Planck they are within 6.4
  per cent of CAMB's at the three maxima and 15 per cent at most in the
  damping tail; the bundle's radiation is photons alone and its damping a
  factor on the transfer, and the tight-coupling transition is still held.
* **CAMB and CLASS are context and not a target** (**S4c**): they share a first
  peak at ℓ ≈ 206 and agree with each other to 0.3 per cent.
