# F11 — the angular autocorrelation as it forms, with the radiation era solved

**v1.4.0's lead figure.** F10's two panels, with both slots reading the
potential solved through the radiation era (`sec:a3-construction`, steps 1 to
3) and the multipole axis carried to $\ell=910$, so that the first three
acoustic maxima are on it.

## What it reads

| | |
|---|---|
| the acoustic series at decoupling | in place, at an eighth of today's height |
| late share at ℓ = 2, 16 | **+0.21, +0.06** |
| late share at ℓ = 90, its largest above ℓ = 30 | **+0.19** |
| late share at the first maximum, ℓ = 210 | **+0.08** |
| late share from ℓ = 325 to 910 | **+0.001 to +0.019** |
| η₀ column against the full integral | 4.5e-14 |

The late share is the fraction of today's C_ℓ laid down after
(η − η\*) = 0.01 (η₀ − η\*), when the visibility has closed. It is positive at
every multipole. On the released potential (F10) it is negative from ℓ = 2
to 25.
