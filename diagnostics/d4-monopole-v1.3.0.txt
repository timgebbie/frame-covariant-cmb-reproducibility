  Annals II p. 366: "there is no monopole temperature anisotropy in
  the CGI approach while there is one in the canonical treatment".

  The covariant hierarchy carries tau^{A_l} for l >= 1, so there is no
  l = 0 object to carry a monopole and none is computed: integral_solution
  refuses ells below 1. What this bundle calls the monopole SECTOR is
  rank 0 of (102) - the C_0 coefficient and the scalar boundary term -
  and the Sachs-Wolfe plateau is its to carry.

  PLANCK_MODEL   r_theta = 3.127387   l_A = pi r_theta / r_s = 266.8

  PHASE, and it is the whole diagnostic:
      rank 0, the monopole, goes as cos(k r_s)  ->  peaks at n l_A        = 267, 534, 800
      rank 1, the dipole,   goes as sin(k r_s)  ->  peaks at (n+1/2) l_A  = 133, 400, 667
      so WHERE the peaks sit says WHICH rank is carrying the spectrum,
      with no reference to any external code.

  k grid derived from T-56: 359 points to k = 120, 3.00 samples per
  j_l^2 oscillation. Everything but the weight and the frame is held fixed.

  C_l by rank, as a fraction of the total. These are AUTO terms and
  C_l is quadratic, so they do not sum to 1 - the remainder is the
  cross terms, reported. F5 makes the same distinction for the same reason.

      The Newtonian frame is FLAT to within 30% over 2 <= l <= 30, which
      is what SOURCE-APPROXIMATIONS-v1.md's A-2 table says e^-kappa gives
      and what a Sachs-Wolfe plateau is. The CDM frame rises by a factor
      of 11 over the same range.

      This is NOT the weight and it is NOT the grid: both columns above
      are one environment, one acoustic solution, one k grid, differing
      only in the frame (110) specialises to. In the Newtonian frame the
      rank-0 share is identically zero - (110) puts the whole source in
      rank one - and the plateau is there anyway, because the monopole
      combination (dT + Phi_A) sits INSIDE c_1 as a_b1 = k(dT + Phi_A).
      In the CDM frame rank 0 carries 99% and the plateau is not there.

      **F9 draws the CDM frame.** So does the released spectrum.

      Annals II's own statement, sources.py: the Newtonian frame carries
      its whole O(l) source in the ACCELERATION pair and the CDM frame
      in the SHEAR pair - two expressions for one operator - and the sum
      is an observable. These two are not the same observable. That is
      finding T-14 and decision S21, now measured without the weight
      masking it: F2 reports the cost of sigma -> 0 in the CDM frame as
      99.9% where it read 0.27% before T-57, and F5 reports the two
      frames' C_l agreeing to a median of 1.00e+00 - that is, not at all.

      **Which frame F9 should draw is the PI's call**, because it is
      Paper 1's claim and not a numerical one. What is not a judgement
      call: the Newtonian frame is the one validated against an analytic
      arbiter - D5, 0.26% against the corrected (201) over 2 <= l <= 20,
      through pipeline.run, which is a Newtonian-frame assembly.
