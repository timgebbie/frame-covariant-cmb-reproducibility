D10 - what approximation A-3 costs: the potential that does not decay
Generated by scripts/diagnostic_d10_radiation_driving.py. Do not edit by hand.

  `potentials` holds Phi CONSTANT below its matter-domination reference,
  which is the recombination grid's first point (z_max = 3000). Since
  T-51 every mode starts far earlier than that. So the oscillator is
  driven across the radiation era by a frozen potential, where the truth
  is the closed form Phi = 3 Phi_i j_1(x)/x, x = k eta / sqrt(3), which
  decays as x^-2 while the mode oscillates. That resonance is the Hu &
  Sugiyama amplification and it is what A-3 discards.

  --- how to read the columns ------------------------------------
      x at eta_eq      k eta_eq / sqrt(3): how far inside the sound
                       horizon the mode already is at equality. Above
                       ~1 the mode oscillated while Phi was decaying,
                       which is where the resonant driving lives.
      Phi_rad(eta_eq)  what the closed form says Phi has fallen to by
                       equality. The released driving uses 1.0000.
      A-3 omits        1 - that: the decay the frozen potential misses.
                       It is a LOWER bound on what A-3 is worth, because
                       a net factor is not the resonance -- the driving
                       is Phi CHANGING while the mode rings, and a
                       constant rescale of Phi rescales delta_T exactly
                       and teaches nothing.
      grid pts below   how many of the mode's acoustic grid points lie
                       below equality. THIS IS WHY THE OSCILLATOR
                       COMPARISON IS NOT HERE.

  --- what this diagnostic deliberately does not do ----------------
      It does not drive the oscillator twice and report a ratio. That
      was the first draft and it failed: on PLANCK_MODEL at k = 10 a
      spliced Phi differing by 1.3e-03 moved delta_T(eta_*) from -0.751
      to +1.190, a sign flip on a 0.13% input, because the acoustic grid
      carries three points below equality and a splice on three points
      is a step in Phi-prime. The oscillator responds to the kink.

      Driving through a resolved radiation era is A-3's FIX and needs a
      grid derived for that era, not for the acoustic one. The columns
      above say which modes need it and how badly, which is what a
      scoping diagnostic is for.

      The Einstein-de Sitter block is the control and it is a no-op by
      construction: Omega_r = 0 makes matter_domination_eta return 0,
      nothing lies below the splice, and the decay factor is
      Phi_rad(0) = 1. An earlier draft spliced at the grid reference
      instead of equality and moved that control by factors of 7 to 10,
      on a background with no radiation era at all. The control caught
      it on the first run, which is the whole reason it is here.
